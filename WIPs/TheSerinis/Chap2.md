“-and I mean, I guess that’s just what I think. I just don’t get it. Maybe, I’m just tired of fish, you know? It’s just, overrated. I guess.” Maddy looked up at the boy, her eyes pinched a little and she tucked her lips in. “I’m sorry, what did you want to buy?”

He chuckled a bit, staring at the ground. “The gobies. All of them. If you have some sole, I’d take that to.”

She silently collected the fish and handed them over. She was deep in thought.

He’d already paid, so when he lingered after she handed him the fish, she looked up at him in irritated confusion.

“I-I make other stuff too. We don’t havbe fish all the time. Rice and peas. Stews. I’ve even started making my own mulled wines. Well, mixing the spices at least.”

“Yeah?” She challenged.

“Maybe if we come by this way again to restock, maybe you and I can go to that bakery?”

She gasped. “You promise?”

He chuckled again. “Yeah, I guess.”

“Ok. I’ll be looking for you.”

He seemed like he wanted to say something, but just nodded and backed away.

Maddy wiped her hands on a rag. She hated wiping the fish smell off on her apron. It made her smell like fish all day.

Well, with her uncle being a fisherman and all, she felt like she absolutely always smelled like fish, it just made her feel more smelly.
