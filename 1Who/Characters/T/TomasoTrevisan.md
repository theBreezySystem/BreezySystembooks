# Character Inspiration and Development

I wanted one of the drowned to have their own line or group
<br> and as a supercargo, Alexis had a great opportunity to 
<br> to help people by training them. Tomaso was the hyper
<br> child that asked "why" about everything. 
<br> I wanted him to reflect someone that was getting something
<br> from The Reed in a human way. He's not going to end up being
<br> a vampire, but he is also a beloved person to the crew.
<br> I wanted a non-tragic child who sees his family a lot.
<br> An apprentice who is someone learning a trade, still with
<br> strong connections to his loving family. 
<br> I wanted him to be the entry of a pleasant ripple from 
<br> The Reed.
